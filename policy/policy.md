# Live Chat Community Policy

This policy applies to every message posted in a live-stream chat. It exists to keep
streamers and viewers safe from scams, abuse and harmful content while preserving the
fast, jokey, competitive culture of live chat. When in doubt, consider the context of a
live stream: banter between friends and gaming trash talk are normal; targeted abuse,
fraud and threats are not.

Each message gets exactly one **label** (the most serious applicable category) and a
**severity**:

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No violation |
| 1 | Low: borderline or mild; a gentle reminder is enough |
| 2 | Medium: clear violation; the message should not be shown |
| 3 | High: severe or dangerous; remove immediately and consider account action |

---

## benign
Normal chat: reactions, questions, compliments, jokes, emotes, mild profanity that is
not aimed at anyone ("this boss is so damn hard"), gaming trash talk inside the game
context ("I'm gonna destroy you next round", "gg ez"), and links the streamer
themselves has shared or asked for.

## scam_spam
Attempts to defraud viewers or pull them off-platform, and disruptive spam.
- **Severity 3:** phishing or fraud: "free coins/gifts/diamonds" offers, fake giveaways,
  "verify your account" links, crypto/investment "double your money" schemes, or
  impersonating the streamer or the platform.
- **Severity 2:** soliciting off-platform contact: "DM me", Telegram/WhatsApp/Signal
  handles, phone numbers, unsolicited links, or follower/view selling.
- **Severity 1:** low-grade spam: repeated characters or emoji floods, all-caps
  shouting, repeated self-promotion ("check out my channel").
- Not a violation: a viewer mentioning a public, well-known site in conversation
  ("I watched the VOD on YouTube").

## harassment
Insults, demeaning or degrading comments aimed at the streamer, a viewer or another
person; bullying; unwanted sexual comments about a person; encouraging others to pile on.
- **Severity 3:** sustained or severe degradation, doxxing (sharing personal info),
  or coordinated attacks.
- **Severity 2:** direct insults or demeaning remarks ("you're a worthless idiot").
- **Severity 1:** rude or mildly mocking remarks aimed at someone ("lol you're bad at this").
- Not a violation: self-deprecation, playful banter that is clearly mutual, or
  criticism of gameplay that isn't personal ("that was a bad play").

## hate
Attacks on people based on protected attributes: race, ethnicity, national origin,
religion, caste, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, disability or serious disease.
Includes slurs, dehumanizing comparisons, and hateful stereotypes.
- **Severity 3:** dehumanization, calls for exclusion or violence, or slurs used as attacks.
- **Severity 2:** hateful stereotypes or derogatory generalizations.
- **Severity 1:** coded or borderline remarks.
- Not a violation: discussing identity, reporting or condemning hate, or in-group
  reclaimed use that is clearly not an attack.

## sexual
Sexually explicit content, sexual solicitation, or sexualizing people in the stream.
Any sexual content involving minors is always severity 3.
- **Severity 3:** explicit sexual content, solicitation, or anything involving minors.
- **Severity 2:** graphic sexual comments.
- **Severity 1:** crude innuendo.

## threat_violence
Threats of violence against real people, incitement, glorification of real violent
attacks, or encouragement of dangerous acts.
- **Severity 3:** credible, specific threats ("I know where you live, I'm coming tonight").
- **Severity 2:** violent threats without specifics ("someone should beat you up").
- **Severity 1:** ambiguous violent language aimed at a real person.
- Not a violation: clearly in-game or figurative language ("I'm gonna kill you in
  this round", "this song slaps", "I'm dead 😂").

## self_harm
Messages about suicide, self-harm or eating disorders.
- Messages expressing personal distress ("I want to die", "I've been cutting again")
  are labeled self_harm so a human can respond with support resources. **They are
  never punished.** The user_message should be supportive and point to help.
- Encouraging another person to self-harm ("kys", "go kill yourself") is labeled
  **harassment, severity 3**, not self_harm.
- Hyperbole ("I'm dying of laughing", "this level makes me want to die lol") is benign.

---

## Choosing between labels
If several categories apply, choose the one with the highest severity; on a tie use this
order: threat_violence > self_harm > hate > sexual > harassment > scam_spam.

## Messages to users
When a message is actioned, the user sees a short note (one or two sentences) that:
- names the rule in plain language,
- does not repeat the offending content or shame the user,
- for self_harm, is warm and points to support instead of citing a rule.

Example: "Sharing outside contact info or links isn't allowed in chat. It helps protect
viewers from scams."
